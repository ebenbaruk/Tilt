# Tilt: the casino you play while your AI codes

Developers now spend hours watching Claude Code or Codex work. The agent reads, thinks, runs the tests… and meanwhile the human waits in front of the terminal. That dead time has become one of the most captive moments in a developer’s day, and nobody owns it yet.

Tilt turns that waiting time into a game. Blackjack, roulette, slots: everything shows up right inside the Claude Code and Codex interface, with no tool or tab switching. You kick off a task, play a hand, the agent finishes, you get back to your code. The game is 100% free to play, but the winnings are real: at the end of each season, the top players split a cash prize pool.

That prize pool is funded by sponsors. Brands buy a visibility slot and appear front and center on the tables, the machines and the win screens, in front of an audience that is extremely hard to reach any other way: developers, builders, early AI adopters, at the exact moment they’re focused on their screen. Part of the sponsor’s spend goes back to the players, so the brand is tied to a moment of winning rather than to an ad people skip.

Tilt is built to spread. Every big win generates a shareable card for X or TikTok, the season leaderboard is public, and every new season restarts the race. Degen culture is already part of tech; Tilt finally gives it a playground, built into the tools developers use every day.

For players, it’s a risk-free casino with real winnings. For sponsors, it’s ad space at the heart of the developer workflow. For us, it’s the first entertainment platform built for the age of AI agents.
